Structure: The company is owned by Justin, with a small year-round staff of six to seven people. The staff includes maintenance personnel and office staff divided among multiple companies owned by Justin.

It operates two locations, with a helipad in a prime location next to a Longhorn Steak House and a bank. Destin Helicopters has a robust financial performance, with increasing margins and revenue growth every year. The business is well-positioned for expansion, with opportunities in nearby tourism markets like Panama City.
