The business structure involves multiple entities with five brothers owning equal shares of 20% each in one entity and four LLCs with 25% ownership in another. John Hepworth is the active manager of Monkey Rock, overseeing its operations.

Business Summary
Jungle Reef and Monkey Rock are interactive entertainment venues located in The Shoppes at Solana, El Paso, TX. They offer a blend of educational and entertaining activities, including guided tours, field trips, and immersive aquatic experiences. The businesses have seen significant growth with a strong focus on family entertainment, leveraging the 'Park Hopper' deal for increased visitor numbers. Despite initial financial challenges due to high upfront costs and leasing issues, the businesses are now profitable with a steady revenue stream. The combination of both venues presents an attractive opportunity for buyers, offering a comprehensive entertainment package with potential for further investment in equipment and marketing.

Growth History
The businesses have experienced steady growth since their inception. The 'Park Hopper' deal has been particularly successful in attracting more visitors. However, initial financial difficulties due to high upfront costs and leasing issues have been overcome through strategic management and cost-cutting measures.

Reason for Sale
The businesses are being sold due to initial financial challenges and the desire to recover capital invested in Monkey Rock. The current owners believe that another entity would be in a better position to manage and improve the businesses.
