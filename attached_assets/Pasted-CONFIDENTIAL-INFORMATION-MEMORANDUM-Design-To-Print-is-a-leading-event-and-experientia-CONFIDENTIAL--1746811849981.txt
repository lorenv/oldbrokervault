BUSINESS OVERVIEW
Founded: 1995
Structure: Design To Print is owned by Josh Bevans, who has 100% ownership. The company
has two production facilities: one in Southern Utah and one in Las Vegas. The Utah facility is
owned outright, while the Las Vegas facility is leased.
Business Description:
Design To Print is a leading event and experiential company specializing in large-scale
installations for events throughout Las Vegas and beyond. Founded in 1995, the company has
grown from a small copy store to a major player in the industry, known for its unique fabric
printing capabilities and custom extrusions. With 75% of its business in events and experiential
activations, Design To Print has diversified into retail with significant partnerships in the industry.
The company is owned by Josh Bevans, who has been instrumental in its growth and innovation.
