Using Website Analysis to Auto-populate Your CIM

4 min read
All Levels

Save time and ensure accuracy by letting CIM Share's AI automatically extract business information from your website. This powerful feature can populate your CIM with company details, services, team information, and more—all with just a URL.

How Website Analysis Works

CIM Share's AI-powered website analyzer crawls your business website to extract relevant information that can be used to create or enhance your CIM. The system intelligently identifies and organizes:

Company Information

Business name, founding date, location, and company overview

Products & Services

Detailed descriptions of offerings, pricing models, and value propositions

Team & Leadership

Key personnel, executive bios, and organizational structure

Market Position

Industry focus, target markets, and competitive advantages

Contact Details

Business address, phone numbers, and contact information

Visual Assets

Logos, product images, and other visual elements

When to Use Website Analysis

Starting from Scratch

If you don't have a transcript or existing content, website analysis provides an excellent starting point for your CIM. It creates a comprehensive foundation that you can then enhance with specific financial data and strategic information.

Enhancing Existing Content

Already have a CIM started from a transcript? Website analysis can fill in gaps and add details that may not have been covered in your meetings, such as complete product listings or team member profiles.

Ensuring Accuracy

Use website analysis to verify and update information in your CIM, ensuring all company details, contact information, and service descriptions are current and accurate.

Good to Know

Website analysis works best with well-structured business websites that have clear About, Services, and Team pages. E-commerce sites, blogs, and portfolio sites are all supported.

Step-by-Step Guide

1
Access Website Analysis

You can use website analysis in two ways:

  • When creating a new CIM: Select "Analyze Website" as your input method
  • For existing CIMs: Click the "Analyze Website" button in the editor toolbar
2
Enter Your Website URL

Type or paste your business website URL into the input field. Make sure to include the full URL including https:// or http://.

Examples:

  • https://www.yourbusiness.com
  • https://yourbusiness.com
  • https://shop.yourbusiness.com
3
Configure Analysis Options

Before starting the analysis, you can customize what information to extract:

  • Depth Level: Choose between Quick Scan (main pages only) or Deep Scan (all accessible pages)
  • Focus Areas: Select specific information types to prioritize
  • Include Images: Toggle whether to extract and include visual assets
Processing Time

Website analysis typically takes 45-90 seconds depending on site size. Large websites with many pages may take up to 3 minutes for a deep scan.

4
Start the Analysis

Click Analyze Website to begin. You'll see a progress indicator showing which pages are being processed. The analyzer will:

  1. Crawl your website's public pages
  2. Extract relevant business information
  3. Organize content into CIM sections
  4. Generate professional descriptions
5
Review and Merge Results

Once analysis is complete, you'll see a preview of the extracted information. You can:

  • Accept All: Add all extracted content to your CIM
  • Select Sections: Choose specific sections to include
  • Edit Before Adding: Modify the content before merging

What Gets Extracted

Automatic Extraction

The AI automatically identifies and extracts these common elements:

  • Company name and tagline
  • Business description and mission statement
  • Product and service offerings
  • Team member names and roles
  • Contact information and locations
  • Client testimonials and case studies
  • Awards and certifications
  • Press mentions and media coverage

Smart Content Organization

The extracted information is automatically organized into standard CIM sections:

  • Executive Summary: Synthesized from various website elements
  • Company Overview: About page content and company history
  • Products & Services: Detailed offerings from service pages
  • Management Team: Leadership information from team pages
  • Market Position: Industry focus and competitive advantages
Pro Tip

For best results, ensure your website has updated content on key pages like About Us, Services, Team, and Contact. The AI performs better with well-structured HTML and clear navigation.

Optimizing Your Website for Analysis

Website Best Practices

  1. Clear Page Structure: Use proper headings (H1, H2, H3) to organize content
  2. Descriptive Content: Include detailed descriptions of your business and services
  3. Updated Information: Ensure all company details are current
  4. Accessible Pages: Make sure important pages aren't behind login walls
  5. Meta Descriptions: Add meta tags for better content identification

Content to Highlight

  • Unique value propositions on your homepage
  • Detailed service descriptions with benefits
  • Team member bios with experience and expertise
  • Client success stories and case studies
  • Industry awards and recognitions

Combining Website Analysis with Other Features

Website + Transcript Combo

For the most comprehensive CIM, combine both methods:

  1. Start with website analysis for foundational information
  2. Upload a transcript to add strategic insights and financial details
  3. The AI will intelligently merge both sources

Iterative Enhancement

You can run website analysis multiple times on the same CIM:

  • After updating your website with new content
  • To capture seasonal changes or new offerings
  • When adding new team members or locations

Troubleshooting Common Issues

Website Not Loading

Issue: Analysis fails to start or returns an error
Solutions:

  • Verify the URL is correct and accessible
  • Check if the website requires login or has access restrictions
  • Ensure the site isn't blocking automated crawlers
  • Try using the main domain instead of specific pages

Missing Information

Issue: Important details aren't being extracted
Solutions:

  • Use Deep Scan mode for more thorough analysis
  • Check if content is in JavaScript-rendered sections
  • Ensure information is in text format, not just images
  • Manually add missing details after analysis

Incorrect Information

Issue: Extracted content contains errors
Solutions:

  • Review and edit content before accepting
  • Use the selective merge option
  • Update your website source content first
  • Report persistent issues to support
Privacy Note

Website analysis only accesses publicly available information. Private pages, password-protected content, and internal systems remain secure and are not accessed.
