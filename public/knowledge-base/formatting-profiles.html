Choosing the Right Formatting Profile

5 min read
All Levels

Your CIM's formatting profile sets the tone and structure for your entire document. Understanding the differences between Balanced, Professional, and Memo styles helps you create documents that resonate with your specific audience and achieve your business goals.

Overview of Formatting Profiles

CIM Share offers three distinct formatting profiles, each optimized for different audiences and use cases. The profile you choose affects how the AI structures content, the level of detail included, and the overall presentation style.

Most Popular
Balanced Profile
The versatile choice that combines narrative flow with structured information
✓ Mix of paragraphs and bullets
✓ Moderate detail level
✓ Easy to scan and read
✓ Flexible for all industries

Key achievements include:

  • 40% year-over-year revenue growth
  • ISO 9001:2015 certification maintained

Professional Profile
Formal, detailed narrative for sophisticated investors and corporate buyers
✓ Paragraph-heavy format
✓ Comprehensive details
✓ Formal business language
✓ Investment-grade presentation

The organization's commitment to continuous improvement has resulted in sustained revenue growth of forty percent year-over-year, while simultaneously achieving an industry-leading customer retention rate of eighty-five percent.

Choosing Based on Your Audience

Quick Decision Guide

  • Private Equity Firms: Professional (detailed due diligence expected)
  • Strategic Buyers: Balanced (mix of strategy and details)
  • Individual Investors: Balanced (accessible yet comprehensive)
  • Business Brokers: Memo (quick evaluation needed)
  • Investment Banks: Professional (formal presentation required)
  • Family Offices: Balanced or Professional (depends on sophistication)
  • Lending Institutions: Professional (detailed financial focus)

Industry-Specific Recommendations

Technology & SaaS

Recommended: Balanced or Memo

Tech buyers often prefer concise, data-driven presentations. Use Balanced for growth-stage companies or Memo for early-stage startups where speed matters.

Manufacturing & Industrial

Recommended: Professional

Traditional industries expect comprehensive documentation. The Professional profile provides the detailed operational insights these buyers require.

Retail & E-commerce

Recommended: Balanced

The mix of narrative and metrics works well for retail businesses, allowing you to tell the brand story while presenting key performance indicators.

Healthcare & Medical

Recommended: Professional

Regulatory compliance and operational complexity demand detailed documentation that the Professional profile delivers.

Service Businesses

Recommended: Balanced or Memo

Depends on business size—use Balanced for established firms, Memo for smaller operations or franchise opportunities.

Pro Tip

You can always change your formatting profile after generation. Start with Balanced if unsure, then switch to Professional or Memo based on initial feedback from potential buyers.

Advanced Formatting Strategies

Multi-Audience Approach

Creating multiple versions for different audiences:

  1. Generate initial CIM with Balanced profile
  2. Duplicate the document
  3. Regenerate sections with Professional profile for institutional investors
  4. Create a third version with Memo profile for initial outreach

Hybrid Formatting

Mix profiles within the same document:

  • Use Memo style for Executive Summary (quick overview)
  • Apply Professional format to Financial sections (detailed analysis)
  • Keep Balanced style for Company Overview (storytelling)

Progressive Disclosure

Start with Memo, upgrade to Professional:

  • Initial outreach: Share Memo-formatted teaser
  • Interested parties: Provide Balanced full CIM
  • Serious buyers: Deliver Professional detailed package

Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Using Professional for Small Businesses

Small business buyers may find Professional format overwhelming. The extensive detail can obscure key selling points.

❌ Using Memo for Complex Transactions

Large or complex deals require comprehensive information. Memo format may appear unprofessional or lacking in substance.

❌ Ignoring Cultural Preferences

International buyers may have different expectations. European investors often prefer Professional, while Silicon Valley buyers lean toward Memo.

❌ Inconsistent Formatting After Edits

Manual edits should maintain the chosen profile's style. Don't mix casual language in a Professional document or add lengthy paragraphs to a Memo.

Testing Your Choice

Before finalizing your formatting decision:

The 5-Minute Test

  1. Ask a colleague to review your CIM for 5 minutes
  2. Quiz them on key business metrics and value propositions
  3. If they can't recall crucial information, consider a different profile

A/B Testing

  • Send different profiles to similar buyer groups
  • Track engagement metrics (time on document, download rates)
  • Note which format generates more follow-up questions
  • Adjust based on response quality, not just quantity

Formatting Profile FAQs

Can I change profiles after generation?

Yes, you can regenerate your entire document with a different profile at any time. Your original content and data remain intact.

Do profiles affect financial data presentation?

Profiles primarily affect narrative sections. Financial data, tables, and metrics remain consistent across all profiles.

Which profile is best for international buyers?

Professional profile typically works best for international transactions, as it provides comprehensive details that overcome language and cultural barriers.

Can I customize beyond the three profiles?

While the three profiles are fixed, you can use regeneration prompts to fine-tune individual sections to your exact specifications.
