How to Generate a CIM from Meeting Transcripts

5 min read
Beginner Friendly

Transform your business meetings and conversations into professional Confidential Information Memorandums (CIMs) using CIM Share's AI-powered transcript analysis. This guide will walk you through the entire process, from preparing your transcript to generating and refining your document.

What You'll Need

  • A transcript of your business meeting or conversation (text format)
  • A CIM Share account (free trial or subscription)
  • About 10-15 minutes to review and refine the generated content

Preparing Your Transcript

Before uploading your transcript, ensure it's properly formatted for the best results:

Supported Formats

  • Plain text (.txt files)
  • Copy-pasted text directly into the input field
  • Meeting notes or conversation logs
  • Interview transcripts
Pro Tip

For best results, include speaker names and timestamps in your transcript. This helps the AI better understand the context and extract key information more accurately.

Transcript Best Practices

  1. Include Key Topics: Make sure your transcript covers essential business information like company overview, financials, market position, and growth opportunities
  2. Clean Up Filler Words: While not required, removing excessive "um," "uh," and repetitions can improve AI comprehension
  3. Identify Speakers: Label different speakers (e.g., "CEO:", "CFO:", "Investor:") to preserve important context
  4. Highlight Numbers: Ensure financial figures, dates, and percentages are clearly stated

Step-by-Step Generation Process

1
Create a New Document

After logging into CIM Share, click the Create New CIM button from your dashboard. This will take you to the document creation page.

2
Choose Input Method

Select "Upload Transcript" as your input method. You'll see two options:

  • Paste Text: Copy and paste your transcript directly into the text area
  • Upload File: Click to browse and select a .txt file from your computer
3
Add Business Context (Optional)

Below the transcript input, you'll find optional fields to provide additional context:

  • Business Name: Enter your company name
  • Industry: Specify your business sector
  • Website URL: Add your website for additional AI analysis
Important Note

Transcript processing typically takes 30-60 seconds depending on length. Longer transcripts (over 10,000 words) may take up to 2 minutes.

4
Select Formatting Profile

Choose how you want your CIM formatted:

  • Balanced: Mix of paragraphs and bullet points (recommended for most users)
  • Professional: Formal paragraph-based format
  • Memo: Concise bullet-point style
5
Generate Your CIM

Click the Generate CIM button to start the AI processing. You'll see a progress indicator while the system analyzes your transcript and creates your document.

Understanding the Generated Content

Once processing is complete, your CIM will contain the following sections automatically extracted and formatted from your transcript:

Standard Sections

  • Executive Summary: High-level overview of the business opportunity
  • Company Overview: Business description, history, and current operations
  • Financial Highlights: Key metrics, revenue, EBITDA, and growth trends
  • Market Analysis: Industry context and competitive positioning
  • Growth Opportunities: Expansion potential and strategic initiatives
  • Investment Highlights: Key selling points for potential buyers/investors

Refining Your Generated CIM

After generation, you can enhance your document using several tools:

Regenerate Specific Sections

  1. Click the regenerate icon next to any section heading
  2. Provide specific instructions (e.g., "Make this more concise" or "Add more financial detail")
  3. The AI will recreate just that section based on your feedback

Manual Editing

  • Click directly on any text to edit it inline
  • Use the rich text editor for formatting (bold, italic, lists)
  • Add custom sections using the "Add Section" button

Enhance with Website Data

If you didn't include a website initially, you can still add it later:

  1. Click "Analyze Website" in the toolbar
  2. Enter your business website URL
  3. The AI will scan your site and merge relevant information into your CIM
Best Practice

Always review AI-generated content for accuracy, especially financial figures and specific claims about your business. The AI provides a strong foundation, but your expertise ensures accuracy.

Common Issues and Solutions

Transcript Too Short

If your transcript is under 500 words, the AI may struggle to generate comprehensive content. Solution: Combine multiple meeting transcripts or add website analysis for additional context.

Missing Financial Information

If financial details weren't discussed in the meeting, manually add them using the Financial Metrics section in the editor.

Incorrect Information Extracted

Simply click on the incorrect text and edit it directly, or regenerate the section with clearer instructions.

Next Steps

Once you're satisfied with your generated CIM:

  1. Add Visual Elements: Upload your logo, business images, and select a professional cover image
  2. Include Financial Documents: Attach supporting files like P&L statements or projections
  3. Configure Sharing: Set up password protection and NDA requirements
  4. Export or Share: Download as PDF/Word or share via secure link
