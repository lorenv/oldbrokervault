Regenerating Sections with AI: Tips for Better Results

6 min read
Advanced Techniques

Master the art of AI regeneration to transform good content into exceptional CIM sections. This guide provides advanced techniques and strategies for crafting prompts that deliver precisely the results you need.

Understanding AI Regeneration

The regeneration feature allows you to recreate any section of your CIM with new instructions, giving you fine-grained control over tone, length, detail level, and focus. Think of it as having a professional writer who can instantly rewrite content based on your specific feedback.

How Regeneration Works

When you regenerate a section, the AI:

  1. Analyzes the original source material (transcript or website data)
  2. Considers your specific instructions
  3. Maintains context from surrounding sections
  4. Creates entirely new content following your guidelines
Key Insight

Each regeneration is independent—you can regenerate the same section multiple times with different prompts until you achieve the perfect result. Your document maintains a history of all versions.

The Art of Prompt Engineering

Anatomy of an Effective Prompt

A well-crafted regeneration prompt contains:

  • Clear Direction: What you want changed
  • Specific Details: How it should be changed
  • Context: Why the change is needed
  • Constraints: Any limitations or requirements
Example: Improving an Executive Summary
"Make this more concise and focus on the financial opportunity. Emphasize the 40% year-over-year growth and the $5M revenue milestone. Use a confident, professional tone that appeals to private equity investors. Keep it under 150 words."

Prompt Strategies by Goal

Goal Prompt Strategy Example Keywords
Shorten Content Specify word/sentence limits "Concise", "Brief", "Under 100 words", "3 bullet points"
Add Detail Request specific information "Expand on", "Include examples", "Add statistics", "Elaborate"

Common Regeneration Scenarios

Scenario 1: Too Technical for Investors

Problem

The product description is filled with technical jargon that investors might not understand.

Solution Prompt
"Rewrite this for non-technical investors. Replace technical terms with business benefits. Focus on market advantages and revenue potential rather than technical specifications. Maintain professional tone but make it accessible."

Scenario 2: Missing Key Metrics

Problem

The financial section lacks specific numbers mentioned in the transcript.

Solution Prompt
"Regenerate with all financial metrics from the source material. Include revenue figures, growth percentages, EBITDA, and customer acquisition costs. Present in both narrative and bullet point format. Add year-over-year comparisons."

Scenario 3: Inconsistent Formatting

Problem

Different sections use different formatting styles.

Solution Prompt
"Match the formatting of the Company Overview section. Use short paragraphs followed by bullet points for key details. Start with a strong topic sentence. Maintain consistent verb tense throughout."

Advanced Techniques

Layered Regeneration

Sometimes the best results come from multiple regenerations:

  1. First Pass: Focus on content and accuracy
  2. Second Pass: Refine tone and style
  3. Third Pass: Polish length and formatting

Comparative Prompts

Reference other sections or external examples:

"Rewrite this to match the energy and optimism of the Growth Opportunities section, but maintain the factual precision of the Financial Highlights."

Constraint Stacking

Combine multiple requirements for precise control:

"Create exactly 5 bullet points, each 15-20 words, focusing on competitive advantages. Use active voice and include one quantifiable metric per point."
Regeneration Limits

Free accounts are limited to 2 regenerations per document. Standard and Enterprise accounts have unlimited regenerations. Use them strategically if you're on a free trial.

Do's and Don'ts

✓ Do's

  • Be specific about desired outcomes
  • Include examples when helpful
  • Specify word counts or limits
  • Define your target audience
  • Use industry-specific terminology
  • Request specific data points

✗ Don'ts

  • Use vague instructions like "make it better"
  • Contradict the source material
  • Request information not in the original data
  • Use multiple conflicting instructions
  • Forget to specify formatting preferences
  • Ignore context from other sections

Prompt Templates by Section

Executive Summary

  • For Investors: "Focus on ROI, market opportunity, and exit potential. Include key metrics."
  • For Strategic Buyers: "Emphasize synergies, market position, and operational strengths."
  • For Lenders: "Highlight cash flow stability, assets, and debt service capability."

Financial Highlights

  • Growth Focus: "Emphasize year-over-year growth rates and future projections."
  • Profitability Focus: "Highlight EBITDA margins, profit trends, and operational efficiency."
  • Stability Focus: "Stress consistent revenue, diverse customer base, and predictable cash flow."

Market Analysis

  • Opportunity Driven: "Focus on TAM, market growth rates, and expansion potential."
  • Competitive Position: "Emphasize differentiation, market share, and barriers to entry."
  • Industry Trends: "Highlight favorable trends, regulatory tailwinds, and technological shifts."

Pro Tip

Save successful prompts in a personal document for future use. Building a library of effective prompts will speed up your CIM creation process over time.

Troubleshooting Regeneration Issues

AI Ignoring Instructions

Solution: Break complex prompts into simpler, single-focus instructions. Use imperative language ("Include..." rather than "It would be nice if...").

Lost Important Information

Solution: Explicitly state what to keep: "Maintain all financial figures while shortening the narrative portions."

Inconsistent Results

Solution: Add more constraints and specific examples. The more detailed your prompt, the more consistent the output.
