# Broker Vault - Product Knowledge Base

## What is Broker Vault?

Broker Vault is the all-in-one deal management platform built for business brokers and M&A advisors. It combines AI-powered document generation, a purpose-built CRM, integrated e-signatures, and secure document sharing into a single platform -- replacing the patchwork of generic tools that brokers currently rely on to manage deals from listing to close.

**Website:** brokervault.ai

---

## Who is Broker Vault For?

### Primary Users

- **Business Brokers** who represent sellers of small to mid-market businesses (typically $500K - $50M deal size) and need to manage listings, qualify buyers, generate marketing materials, and close transactions.
- **M&A Advisors** who work on the sell-side or buy-side of mergers and acquisitions and need professional-grade documents, deal tracking, and buyer pipeline management.

### Secondary Users

- **Investment Bankers** working on lower-middle-market transactions who need CIM generation, data room capabilities, and deal pipeline management.
- **Business Owners** who are preparing their company for sale and need professional marketing materials and a structured process.

### What They All Have in Common

These professionals juggle dozens of active deals, hundreds of buyer contacts, and thousands of confidential documents -- all while maintaining strict confidentiality. They need tools that are purpose-built for the way deals actually work, not generic CRMs and document tools retrofitted for M&A.

## Core Features

### 1. AI-Powered CIM Generation

The Confidential Information Memorandum (CIM) is the cornerstone document of any business sale. Broker Vault uses AI to generate professional CIMs in minutes instead of days.

**How it works:**

- Upload a transcript, audio recording, or PDF about the business
- Alternatively, enter the company's website and let Broker Vault automatically extract key information
- The AI analyzes the source material and generates a structured, professional CIM
- Choose from multiple formatting profiles: balanced, professional, memo, robust, or conversational
- Provide section-by-section direction to control tone, emphasis, and content
- Add financial data fields (Asking Price, Revenue, EBITDA, Cash Flow) with visibility toggles
- Generate a cover image or upload your own
- Export as branded PDF, DOCX, or HTML

**Key capabilities:**

- Document versioning and history tracking
- Regenerate individual sections or the entire document
- Custom sections for additional content (text, images, HTML)
- Tag-based organization across your document library
- Rich text editing for manual refinements
- Document locking for collaborative editing with real-time edit indicators

### 2. CRM (Contact & Deal Management)

A purpose-built CRM designed around the way brokers and advisors actually work -- tracking deals through stages, managing buyer and seller relationships, and keeping every interaction documented.

#### Contacts

Full-featured contact records with fields that matter for deal-making:

- Contact types: Buyer, Seller, Advisor, Other
- Lifecycle stages: Lead, Qualified, Opportunity, Customer
- Lead status tracking: New, Contacted, Qualified, Unqualified
- Source tracking (website, referral, cold outreach, event, etc.)
- Company associations and role/title tracking
- Tags for flexible categorization
- Custom properties for any additional data you need
- Profile pictures (auto-fetched from Gravatar/Google or uploaded manually)
- LinkedIn profile linking
- Full activity timeline per contact

#### Companies

First-class company records that connect to contacts and deals:

- Industry classification and company size
- Annual revenue tracking
- Website, domain, phone, and LinkedIn
- Company logo (auto-fetched or uploaded)
- Owner assignment for accountability
- Address and location tracking
- Custom properties
- Associated contacts and deals view

#### Deals & Pipeline Management

Visual, drag-and-drop deal pipeline designed for tracking transactions from engagement to close:

- **Kanban board view** with drag-and-drop deal movement between stages
- Customizable pipelines with configurable stages
- Deal probability scoring (at the stage or individual deal level)
- Expected close date and deal amount tracking (multi-currency)
- Priority levels: Low, Normal, High
- Deal status tracking: Active, Won, Lost
- Owner assignment and collaborator access
- Company and contact associations per deal
- Custom properties for deal-specific data
- Deal rotation tracking (flag stale deals that haven't progressed)
- Saved views with custom filters and column configurations

#### Buyer Pipeline

A secondary pipeline system for tracking multiple buyer prospects within each deal:

- Custom buyer pipeline stages (separate from the main deal pipeline)
- Track each buyer's progress independently
- Buyer-specific notes and follow-up dates
- Contact and company associations per buyer prospect
- Last contact date tracking

#### Pipeline Configuration

- Create multiple pipelines for different deal types or workflows
- Configure stages with terminal states (Won/Lost)
- Set probability percentages per stage
- Define deal rotation thresholds (days before a deal is flagged as stale)
- Set default currency per pipeline

### 4. E-Signature System

A built-in e-signature platform that eliminates the need for DocuSign or similar tools -- purpose-built for the documents brokers send every day.

#### General E-Signatures

- **Template builder** with drag-and-drop field placement on any uploaded document
- Field types: Signature, Initials, Name, Email, Date, Text
- Placeholder recipients with role-based assignments (e.g., "Buyer," "Seller")
- Signing order options: parallel (all at once) or sequential (ordered)
- Envelope tracking: Draft, Sent, Completed, Voided, Declined
- Recent recipient autocomplete for faster sending
- Document hash verification for tamper detection
- Certificate of completion generation
- Comprehensive audit logging (IP address, geolocation, timestamps)
- E-SIGN Act and UETA compliance tracking with consent documentation

#### PowerForms

Self-service signing via shareable public links:

- Create a reusable signing link from any e-signature template
- Share the link on your website, in emails, or anywhere
- Recipients fill in their details and sign without you sending individual envelopes
- Completion tracking and configurable limits
- Ideal for standard NDAs, engagement letters, or intake forms

#### NDA Workflow

A dedicated NDA signing system integrated with document sharing:

- NDA templates with configurable signature fields
- Multi-signer support with field assignments
- Approval workflows (optional manual approval before granting access)
- Stable redirect URLs for consistent sharing
- Access token system for secure, signer-specific document access
- Signature tracking with geolocation and IP logging

## Key Differentiators

1. **Built for brokers, not adapted from generic tools.** Every feature is designed around the M&A deal lifecycle -- from listing to close. No forcing a generic CRM to track deal stages it wasn't designed for.

2. **AI-powered CIM generation.** What used to take days of writing and formatting now takes minutes. Upload a transcript or point to a website, and get a professional CIM ready for review.

3. **Integrated e-signatures and NDA workflow.** No more switching to DocuSign. NDAs, engagement letters, and closing documents are signed right inside the platform, with automatic access control tied to NDA completion.

4. **Dual pipeline system.** Track deals through your sell-side pipeline while simultaneously managing buyer prospects in a dedicated buyer pipeline within each deal.

5. **Secure document sharing with analytics.** Know exactly who viewed your CIM, how long they spent, and whether they downloaded it -- all behind password protection and NDA gates.

6. **Purpose-built financial analysis.** The SDE Analyzer understands the financial metrics that matter for business valuation, not generic accounting reports.

7. **One platform replaces many.** Broker Vault replaces the need for separate CRM, e-signature, document generation, data room, and listing tools -- reducing cost, complexity, and the risk of data silos.

## Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **CIM** | Confidential Information Memorandum -- the primary marketing document for a business sale, containing detailed business, financial, and operational information. |
| **SDE** | Seller's Discretionary Earnings -- a measure of a business's total financial benefit to a single owner-operator, used as the primary valuation metric for small businesses. |
| **NDA** | Non-Disclosure Agreement -- a legal agreement signed by prospective buyers before they can access confidential business information. |
| **Teaser** | A brief, anonymous summary of a business for sale, used to generate buyer interest before requiring an NDA. |
| **Data Room** | A secure virtual repository where confidential documents are stored and shared with authorized parties during due diligence. |
| **Pipeline** | A visual representation of deal stages, from initial engagement through closing, used to track and manage deal progress. |
| **PowerForm** | A self-service e-signature feature that allows recipients to sign documents via a public link without the sender creating individual envelopes. |
| **Deal Rotation** | A metric tracking how long a deal has remained in its current stage, used to identify stale deals that need attention. |
| **Engagement Letter** | A formal agreement between a broker/advisor and their client (the business seller) outlining the terms of the advisory relationship. |
| **LOI** | Letter of Intent -- a document outlining the preliminary terms of a deal between buyer and seller before formal due diligence begins. |
