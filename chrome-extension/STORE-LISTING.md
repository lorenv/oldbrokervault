## Detailed Description

### Transform Any Company Website into a Professional CIM Document

The CIM Generator by BrokerVault lets business brokers, M&A advisors, and investment bankers create professional Company Information Memorandum (CIM) documents instantly from any company website.

**How It Works:**

1. Browse to any company's website
2. Click the CIM Generator icon
3. Click "Generate CIM"
4. Get a professionally formatted CIM document in minutes

**Key Features:**

**One-Click Generation**
No more copying and pasting. Our AI automatically extracts company information, descriptions, and key details from the webpage to create a comprehensive CIM.

**Smart Content Extraction**
The extension intelligently pulls:
- Company name and description
- Business overview information
- Product and service details
- Contact information
- And more...

**Background Processing**
Start generating a CIM and continue browsing. You'll receive a notification when your document is ready to review and edit.

**Seamless Integration**
Works directly with your BrokerVault account. All generated CIMs are automatically saved to your dashboard for easy access, editing, and sharing.

**Professional Output**
Every CIM is formatted professionally with:
- Executive summary
- Business description
- Market analysis
- Operations overview
- And customizable sections

**Who Is This For?**

- Business Brokers preparing listing materials
- M&A Advisors creating deal documents
- Investment Bankers developing pitch materials
- Business Owners preparing sale documentation
- Private Equity professionals researching targets

**Requirements:**
- Active BrokerVault account (free trial available)
- Google Chrome browser

**Getting Started:**

1. Install the extension
2. Click the icon and sign in with your BrokerVault account
3. Navigate to any company website
4. Click "Generate CIM" and let our AI do the work

**Privacy First:**
We only access webpage content when you explicitly click "Generate CIM." We never track your browsing history or collect data without your action. See our privacy policy for full details.

**Support:**

Start creating professional CIM documents in seconds. Install the CIM Generator today!

## Justification for Permissions

If Chrome asks for permission justifications during review:

**activeTab**: Required to read the current page's content when the user clicks "Generate CIM"

**storage**: Required to securely store the user's authentication token

**notifications**: Required to notify users when their CIM document is ready

**scripting**: Required to inject the content script that extracts page information

**identity**: Required for secure OAuth-style authentication with BrokerVault

**host_permissions (https://*/*)**: Required to read webpage content from any company website the user wants to generate a CIM from. The extension only accesses pages when the user explicitly requests CIM generation.
