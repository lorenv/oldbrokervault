## BrokerVault CIM Generator Chrome Extension

This section describes how the BrokerVault CIM Generator Chrome Extension ("Extension") collects, uses, and protects your information.

### Information We Collect

#### Authentication Data
When you sign in to the Extension, we collect:
- Your BrokerVault account email address
- A secure authentication token (stored locally in your browser)

We do **not** collect or store your password in the Extension.

#### Page Content Data
When you choose to generate a CIM document, the Extension collects the following information from the webpage you are currently viewing:
- Page title
- Meta description
- Open Graph metadata (title, description, image URL)
- Schema.org structured data (if present)
- Main text content of the page

This data is collected **only** when you explicitly click the "Generate CIM" button. The Extension does not automatically collect data from pages you visit.

#### Device Information
We collect basic device information for security purposes:
- Browser type and version (from User-Agent header)
- Operating system

### How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:
- Authenticate your identity and maintain your session
- Generate CIM documents based on the webpage content you provide
- Improve our AI-powered document generation capabilities
- Provide customer support and troubleshoot issues

### Data Storage and Security

- **Authentication tokens** are stored securely in Chrome's encrypted sync storage and are automatically synchronized across your devices where you're signed into Chrome
- **Page content** is transmitted securely via HTTPS to our servers for processing
- **Generated documents** are stored in your BrokerVault account and subject to our standard data retention policies
- We do not sell your data to third parties

### What We Do NOT Collect

The Extension does **not**:
- Track your browsing history
- Collect data from pages you visit without your explicit action
- Access your bookmarks, downloads, or other browser data
- Run in the background when not actively being used
- Collect personal information from the websites you visit beyond what is needed to generate your CIM

### Data Retention

- Authentication tokens expire after 30 days of inactivity
- Page content used for CIM generation is processed and not retained separately from your generated documents
- You can revoke access at any time by signing out of the Extension

### Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:
- **Access**: View and export your generated CIM documents through your BrokerVault account
- **Delete**: Remove generated documents and revoke Extension access at any time
- **Opt-out**: Uninstall the Extension to stop all data collection

### Third-Party Services

The Extension communicates exclusively with BrokerVault servers (brokervault.ai). We use the following third-party services for Extension functionality:
- **Chrome Identity API**: For secure authentication flow
- **Chrome Storage API**: For secure local storage of authentication tokens

### Children's Privacy

The Extension is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy on our website and updating the "Last Updated" date.
